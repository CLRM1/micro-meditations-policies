Micro Meditations Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Micro Meditations is built to work entirely on your device. The short version: the app collects no data, sends no data, and has no way to identify you.

What the app collects

Nothing. Micro Meditations has no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests at all. Every meditation and audio file ships inside the app.

What stays on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information in your phone's local storage so it can remember where you left off:

  • A record of sessions you've completed
  • Whether you've seen the intro screen

This information never leaves your device. It isn't transmitted anywhere, and I have no way to see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the change will be called out in the App Store release notes.
