Micro Meditations

Micro Meditations is a meditation training app for iPhone: short guided meditations, around two minutes or less, designed to teach you to meditate on your own, without your phone. No account, no streaks, no notifications. Open it when you need it.

Before you start

Micro Meditations is for general relaxation and wellbeing. It isn't medical care and isn't a substitute for it. Breathing exercises should feel easy: if you ever feel lightheaded, short of breath, or uncomfortable, stop the meditation and let your breathing return to normal. Never force a breath or a hold.
